Glorp: Snack Family

Privacy Policy · Last updated 20 November 2026 · Applies to the iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play) versions

The short version

Glorp does not collect any data. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no chat. Everything you do in the game stays on your phone.

What stays on your device

Your progress (coins, family, home, garden, collection, scores and settings) is saved only on your phone, in the app's private storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

On Android, if you have turned on Google's device backup, Android may include this saved progress in your own backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under your Google Account, not by us, and we cannot access it. On iPhone, the same applies to your iCloud or computer backups.

Notifications

If you choose to turn on reminders, Glorp schedules notifications on your phone (for example, "your crop is ready"). They are created locally on the device; nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off at any time in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Sharing

If you tap Share, your phone shows its standard share sheet with an image or text of your score. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it.

Children

Glorp is made for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Purchases

Glorp offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs, a Starter Pack and a Coin Doubler). On iPhone, payments are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. On Android, payments are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. We never see your name, payment details, Apple Account or Google Account. The game only receives confirmation from the store that a purchase was made, so it can add your coins on your phone. Connecting to the store for purchases is the only reason the game uses the internet.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
