Glorp: Snack Family

Support · iPhone and Android

How to play

Hold the screen to open Glorp's mouth and let go to close it. Eat the good snacks, keep your mouth shut when junk flies in, and grab coins. Every snack you eat feeds Glorp's family and builds their home.

Common questions

How do I pause? Tap the ⏸ button in the top-left corner during a run. The game also pauses by itself if you leave the app.

How do I get more coins? Play runs, finish daily and weekly challenges, claim badges, and harvest the garden.

Can I buy coins? Yes, optionally. Tap ＋ on the coin counter to see coin packs, the Starter Pack and the Coin Doubler. Coins can always be earned by playing too.

I bought something on a new phone. How do I get it back? The Coin Doubler and Starter Pack can be restored: sign in with the same Apple Account or Google Account, open the shop and tap Restore purchases. Coin packs are used up once added and can't be restored.

A purchase went wrong. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google Play. To ask for a refund on iPhone, visit reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, see Google Play refunds. Or email us below.

What is the Daily Feast? One run per day that is the same for every player. You get one go, then you can share your result.

How do I turn off sounds, vibration or reminders? Tap ⚙️ on the main screen.

Android: the back button pauses a run (press it again to resume) and leaves the results screen.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your phone. Deleting the app removes it, unless your phone's own backup (iCloud, or Google backup on Android) restores it.
